# Separate donations from usable blood components

The professor approved replacing the original immediate-availability model with separate donations and components, including laboratory quarantine. Components retain versioned product profiles, source links and individual expiries; result revisions and release decisions are append-only. Old records are retained as unknown legacy products and stay quarantined until explicitly verified, rather than fabricating a product type or clearance history.

The existing single inventory lock serializes processing, laboratory changes and issuance together. It limits write throughput but keeps concurrent safety checks and audit rollback straightforward for this individual student project. Clinical cross-matching, full recall and sensor-based cold-chain monitoring remain outside the approved scope.
